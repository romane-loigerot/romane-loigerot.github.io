T'as la recette

-BOOK OF RECIPES-
collection editoriale
April to May 2025

A series of cookbooks for students. Through their covers, they should convey something simple to make and accessible in terms of both budget and cooking level.
I created my own pictograms, trying to give them a fun and playful aspect, which contrasts with the purely functional use of pictograms in everyday life. They had to represent the spirit of the book by themselves.
After observing many cookbooks with photos of dishes on the cover, I decided to create a simplified visual, as I felt this was rather limiting for a book containing sixty recipes.
