Larme lyrique

-MANIFESTE-
Risograph prints, blue ink on red paper and red ink on white paper. This project was completed during a workshop led by graphic designer Hadrien Herzog.
April 2025

In response to the evictions that occurred at the Théâtre de La Gaîté Lyrique in March 2025, this manifesto recounts some of the events and takes a stance against the violence directed at immigrants.
Here, the graphic design is intended to be both assertive and provocative, giving a face to this cry of protest.
